About the AuthorMikael Yemane

About the Author

I build machine-learning systems that other people depend on: predictive maintenance for aircraft, wildfire risk on the electrical grid, revenue forecasting and sales intelligence for enterprise software.

Today I lead an ML team building predictive-maintenance systems for commercial aviation. I like owning a problem end to end, from the data pipeline to the model to the decision someone makes with it.

Practitioner Lens · Why a math book

I have onboarded a lot of ML engineers. The ones who struggled were not short on tooling; they were short on the math underneath it. The book is the one I kept wishing I could hand them.

Work

Aerospace

Principal ML Engineer, Team Lead · predictive maintenance · current

I lead a cross-functional team of ML, MLOps and data engineers building predictive-maintenance systems for airline fleets. The work runs end to end, from data pipelines and deployment infrastructure to the alerts maintenance crews act on. Most of it is making models dependable enough to run a fleet on.

2

Pacific Gas & Electric

Senior Data Scientist · energy · wildfire risk

I built computer-vision models over drone imagery of California’s electrical grid to flag deteriorating equipment and wildfire-ignition risk. Distributed pipelines turned raw drone video into per-asset risk scores that decided where inspection crews went first.

3

Microsoft

Senior ML Engineer · sales intelligence

I built sales-intelligence and lead-ranking systems used by thousands of sellers. They combined geographic, firmographic, technographic and behavioral signals into production pipelines that ranked high-value prospects across the product portfolio.

4

Okta

Senior ML Engineer · revenue forecasting

I built churn and ARR forecasting models across the revenue base and took them to production, improving performance by 13% to 97% predictive accuracy. I presented the results to sales leadership and the C-suite as decisions on at-risk accounts and retention.

5

Accenture

ML Engineer, Technical Lead · federal services

I led MLOps for production models at a federal agency, with CI/CD pipelines serving millions of API calls a day. I refactored petabyte-scale ETL on Spark and Databricks and redesigned a model architecture that doubled performance while cutting AWS spend roughly in half.

6

Booz Allen Hamilton

Data Scientist · defense aviation

I built the prototype deep-learning predictive-maintenance system for the U.S. Navy’s F/A-18 Super Hornet fleet. It saved more than $2M, and the prototype won a new contract worth more than $10M. I also built an ML classifier for Navy air-traffic-control maintenance workflows.
